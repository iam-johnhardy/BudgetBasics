About BudgetBasics

Money basics, made approachable

BudgetBasics is a beginner-focused learning project about building clearer, more thoughtful money habits.

Our purpose

Help people feel more prepared to make everyday money decisions.

We bring budgeting lessons and simple planning exercises together in one place. The aim is to make core ideas easier to understand and practice, one step at a time.

Start with the basics

Learn how income, expenses, saving, and spending choices relate to a personal budget.

Practice at your own pace

Use the educational tools to explore examples and organize your own plans locally in your browser.

Know the limits

BudgetBasics is not a bank, payment service, external AI provider, or replacement for professional financial advice.
