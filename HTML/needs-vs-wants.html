Module 03

Needs vs Wants

Pause before spending and decide whether a purchase supports your essentials or your preferences.

Needs

Essential costs that support health, safety, work, or school.

Rent, basic groceries, medicine, transport to class

Wants

Optional purchases that add comfort, fun, or convenience.

Takeout, new headphones, streaming upgrades, décor

Try it

Classify this item: A weekly grocery shop

Decision guide

Before you buy a non-essential

  1. Pause: wait 24 hours before buying.
  2. Check: can you pay without using money for needs?
  3. Choose: will you still value it next week?
  4. Decide: buy it only when the answer is a confident yes.
